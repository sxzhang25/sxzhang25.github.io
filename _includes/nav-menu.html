{%- comment -%} Sub-pages of include.parent, as
  • items, followed (recursively) by their own sub-pages, one indent level deeper. A page counts as a sub-page if it sets `nav_order` in its front matter and its URL is directly under include.parent (e.g. /visuals/highlights/ under /visuals/). Siblings are sorted by nav_order. Levels alternate upright/italic: the top-level nav is upright, so its sub-pages (odd depths) get class "italic", their sub-pages are upright, etc. Items get class "in-trail" when they are the current page or one of its ancestors, and "current" when they are the current page. Usage: {% include nav-menu.html parent="/visuals/" depth=1 %} (Liquid variables are global, so everything is recomputed from include.* on each iteration rather than kept from before a recursive call.) {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign menu_pages = site.pages | where_exp: "p", "p.nav_order" | sort: "nav_order" -%} {%- for child in menu_pages -%} {%- assign parent_size = include.parent | split: '/' | size -%} {%- assign child_size = child.url | split: '/' | size -%} {%- assign child_prefix = child.url | slice: 0, include.parent.size -%} {%- assign expected_size = parent_size | plus: 1 -%} {%- if child_size == expected_size and child_prefix == include.parent -%} {%- assign trail_prefix = page.url | slice: 0, child.url.size -%} {%- assign depth_parity = include.depth | modulo: 2 -%}
  • {{ child.title }}
    • {%- assign next_depth = include.depth | plus: 1 -%} {%- include nav-menu.html parent=child.url depth=next_depth -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%}
