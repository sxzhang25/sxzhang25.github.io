Hi! 👋 I'm a PhD student at Stanford University advised by Maneesh Agrawala in the Stanford Graphics + HCI group.

I'm excited by process-driven creative tools that help people bring their visual ideas to life. Some topics that I find interesting are extracting the right structured editable representations for different creative goals and performing visual verification in generative systems.

Previously, I received a B.A. in mathematics from Princeton University, where I was fortunate to work with Olga Russakovsky, Amit Singer, and Amit Moscovich. My work is supported by the Brown Institute for Media Innovation and an NSF Graduate Research Fellowship.

Outside of research, I enjoy painting and seeing as much art as I can! You may also find me swimming, running, biking or watching baseball.
